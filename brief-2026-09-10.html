01 · Matter & Thread / Devices

Philips Hue: all new lights ship Matter over Thread

Hueblog confirmed Sep 9 what the IFA floor implied: every new Philips Hue light, bulb, and light strip supports Matter over Thread, including the Hue × Nanoleaf wall-light controller. Direct Matter pairing is real for simple lamps and outdoor sconces; gradient products still need the Hue app for full color features because Matter does not yet carry gradients.

That sits on top of Signify’s June concurrent multiprotocol plan with Silicon Labs: select MG26 / SiMG301 bulbs will eventually run Zigbee and Matter over Thread at once via a later software update. Today, commission-time choice still applies on those chips. Hue Secure battery camera quietly moved to 2K; Apple Home camera support is still “working on it,” with no date.

Practical takeaway for a local stack: Bridge + Hue app remains the full-feature path; Matter-over-Thread is the interoperability on-ramp for non-gradient fixtures.

Primary source
Hueblog, 9 Sep 2026; Silicon Labs / Signify CMP announcement

02 · Matter / Devices

TP-Link Tapo Matter cameras — hardware revisions end of September

What happened
Heise’s IFA wrap: TP-Link plans end-of-September hardware revisions of Tapo C560WS, C260, and C660 Kit (marked v2.0 / v2.6) with more compute and memory for Matter camera code. Housings stay the same. Ecosystem reality check: among major platforms, only Samsung SmartThings currently speaks Matter cameras; Apple/Google/Amazon are not there yet. Aqara still the only prior Matter camera shipper called out in the piece.
Why it matters
Matter cameras leave the slide deck and get SKUs with a calendar date — still gated by controller support outside SmartThings.
Primary source
heise online IFA smart-home wrap; Tapo C560WS

03 · Matter & Thread

Shelly ThreadLink — full TCP/IP over Thread for Gen4

What happened
Missed in Tuesday’s IFA-close brief: opt-in Gen4 firmware that runs a full TCP/IP stack (UDP + TCP) over Thread so Matter, Shelly Cloud (via border-router NAT64), and peer Shelly RPC can share one low-power radio — no Wi-Fi credentials on the device. Free; roll out ~3 months after the Sep 3 announce (roughly December). Needs a Thread border router for cloud/app; device-to-device scenes stay in-mesh. Dedicated HA module promised beyond Matter’s data model.
Why it matters
Most vendors treat Thread as Matter transport only. Shelly is treating Thread as the network.
Primary source
Shelly ThreadLink media kit
