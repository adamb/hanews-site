Led: takeover of Shelly Group

Shelly Group's board considers the €70/share cash tender offer from Schneider Electric (via subsidiary SE 2026 A SAS) fair and has formally backed it — an independent fairness opinion from Freitag & Co. agrees. The offer is ~27% above the unaffected six-month VWAP (€55.16 on 28 Jul). Regulatory clearance is still pending; if completed, Shelly keeps its Sofia HQ and workforce for at least three years, and a complete offer could end minority liquidity (delisting).

What happened: a global energy-and-buildings giant is buying one of Home Assistant's most popular device vendors (via tender offer, board-supported). Why it matters: Shelly devices are heavily used in HA setups and their local, no-cloud-first design is a big part of the appeal. Watch for integration friction after close, and note Shelly's 2026 line (ThreadLink, Wall Display XL) was already shipping.

Sources: Shelly Group IR, EQS newswire, heise online. Image: Shelly.

Apple's new home hub: HomeView

At the “Welcome Home” event on 13 Oct, Apple announced HomeView: a 7-inch smart home display with Siri AI, up to six user faces, camera dashboard (nine feeds), and Matter accessory support. Also announced: HomePod mini 2 ($149, 30 Oct) and a new Apple TV 4K. HomeView is $299 (tabletop or wall), pre-orders today, ships 13 Nov. Wall mounts are from Schneider Home ($59.95/$79.95; PoE model early 2027).

Why it matters for HA users: it's locked to Apple Home — no Home Assistant bridge, no app installs. Home Assistant will not show up on the dashboard. The one angle: it speaks Matter, and there's a HomeKit Bridge integration path to expose HA entities into Apple Home. Treat that as a workaround, not a platform.

Sources: Apple newsroom (13 Oct), MacRumors, 9to5Mac.
