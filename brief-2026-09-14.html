14 September 2026

Weekend patch drop. Home Assistant Core moves to 2026.9.2; ESPHome beta tips to 2026.9.0b4 this morning; Hue’s Play gradient strip pro hits the Sep 15 ship window. Radio stack otherwise quiet.

01 · Home Assistant

Home Assistant Core 2026.9.2 ships

Friday evening UTC (11 Sep, 19:40) Frenck tagged 2026.9.2 — the patch Friday’s brief was still waiting on. It is a maintenance cut, not a feature dump: ZHA bumps to 2.2.2 / zha-quirks 2.2.2, go2rtc redacts credentials from server logs, ESPHome setup no longer fails when Supervisor is still warming up, Nest climate recomputes supported features from live traits, Tesla Fleet bounds the first vehicle refresh with a timeout, and frontend moves to 20260826.7. Also in the bag: local_todo ICS CRLF repair, remote_calendar timeout/encoding polish, Reolink/Roborock/ViCare/Enphase dependency bumps, and a pile of smaller integration fixes (Vizio auth, webOS source clear, Openhome polling).

Practical takeaway: if you held on 2026.9.1 for the quiet weekend, .2 is the build to take on a weekday morning — especially ZHA and go2rtc operators.

Primary source
home-assistant/core 2026.9.2 (published 2026-09-11T19:40:36Z)

02 · ESPHome

ESPHome 2026.9.0b4 tips the beta

What happened: jesserockz tagged 2026.9.0b4 today (14 Sep, 03:27 UTC). Highlights for builders: better multi-interface network::is_connected(), BLE tracker re-registers GATT clients after disable/enable, Wi-Fi drops a scan instead of aborting when results cannot be allocated, path resolution against the declaring YAML file, and Device Builder bumps through 1.14.9. Stable remains 2026.8.2.

Why it matters: Friday’s brief still said b3 — beta chase moved overnight.

Primary source
esphome/esphome 2026.9.0b4

03 · Devices / Hue

Hue Play gradient strip pro — Sep 15 ship window

What happened: Signify’s Sep 3 IFA press still calendars the Play gradient strip light / strip light pro portfolio for 15 September (from £89.88 / €99.99). Pro adds OmniGlow wall-wash and a 100" SKU; both lines are cuttable. Bridgeless Bluetooth sync with Play Screen Sync or the HDMI sync box 4K stays a one-strip start; Bridge/Bridge Pro unlocks the larger surround set. US product pages still show “Coming soon” this morning — treat tomorrow as the window, not a guaranteed every-SKU drop.

Why it matters: Calendar watch for entertainment lighting, not a new Matter claim — Matter for Hue lighting remains Bridge / third-party hub path as before.
