Sample weekday brief. Five to six scored items: what happened, why it matters, primary source. Not autonomously published.

01 · ESPHome

ESPHome Starter Kit

What happened
ESPHome published its first official product on 12 Aug 2026: a starter kit designed and produced with Apollo Automation, teased in April at State of the Open Home.
Why it matters
The firmware project now has first-party hardware. Local-first modules on an ESP32-C6, majority of profit to the Open Home Foundation.
Primary source
esphome.io, 12 Aug 2026

02 · Home Assistant

2026.8 Approachable by design

What happened
Home Assistant 2026.8 shipped 5 Aug. New OS installs drop :8123. Entity IDs, device registry splits, Z-Wave lock credentials, and UI wording also move.
Why it matters
Onboarding changes in the monthly, not in a side-channel blog. Existing installs keep their port.
Primary source
home-assistant.io/blog 2026.8

03 · Home Assistant

Home Assistant Core 2026.8.0

What happened
The August monthly GitHub tag is 2026.8.0.
Why it matters
Integrations, repairs, and radio-stack work land on the tag. The blog is commentary; the tag is the artifact.
Primary source
github.com/home-assistant/core 2026.8.0

04 · Home Assistant / Matter

Home Assistant Core 2026.8.2

What happened
Patch 2026.8.2 (14 Aug) restores “via device” on composed Matter devices behind bridges. Regression from 2026.8.0.
Why it matters
Bridged multi-endpoint Matter devices had fallen off the bridge’s device page. Single-endpoint bridged devices were fine.
Primary source
PR #179118; tag 2026.8.2

05 · Matter & Thread

Homey Matter 1.5 certified

What happened
CSA certified Homey Pro, Pro mini, and Self-Hosted Server as Matter 1.5 (software component, cert CSA26025SWC60263-M1, 29 Jun 2026, firmware 13.3.0).
Why it matters
A controller-side 1.5 data point. Not a new device type, not a shopping list.
Primary source
Homey; CSA listing

06 · Devices

SwitchBot Circulator Fan 2 Pro

What happened
SwitchBot launched the Battery Circulator Fan 2 Pro in North America on 29 Jul 2026 with Matter over Wi-Fi. No SwitchBot hub required for basic control.
Why it matters
Hub-free Matter on a battery appliance. The radio path is the story.
Primary source
SwitchBot / PR Newswire
