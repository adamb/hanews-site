How the paper is built

An intelligence pipeline, not a blog CMS and not a swarm of agents. Sources in, one short brief out — then a human says yes before anything is public. Home Assistant is a section of the paper, not the name of it.

The pipeline

RSS and GitHub in. Dedupe. Score. Brief. Focused scripts, not a swarm of agents. A human gate before the public site.

01 discover

RSS + GitHub

Poll releases, blogs, and ecosystem posts. Not social firehoses.

02 dedupe

Collapse the copies

The same Home Assistant release shows up as a blog post, a GitHub tag, and six rewrites. Keep one.

03 score

Novelty and relevance

Rank for a running local stack. New core, Matter, ESPHome, or a break beats a retailer roundup.

04 brief

One short file

What happened, why it matters, primary source. The pipeline logs every keep and reject.

Briefs are human-approved before they become the public site. There is no autonomous publishing.

Cadence

Weekdays, morning (America/Puerto_Rico):

  1. Daily brief — THE BRIEFING rail + /brief/YYYY-MM-DD. 5–10 scored items. What happened, why it matters, primary source.
  2. Daily news — if a story clears the bar, one short reported post in the center well (not a rewrite of the press release).

Weekly (Monday):

  1. One evergreen explainer — the thing a subscriber still needs in six months (Matter 1.5, Thread border routers, ESPHome kit, Home Assistant release changes).

Email: the list gets the daily brief every weekday once signup is live. Weekly explainer goes in Monday’s letter. The list is the asset. Quality over volume. Log every keep/reject in the pipeline (already true in the repo).

Coverage

Home automation — Home Assistant, Matter, Thread, ESPHome, Zigbee/Z-Wave, local-first hardware, energy, presence. Not broad consumer-tech, not retailer roundups.
