15 September 2026

Ship-day check on Hue’s Play gradient strip pro: Signify’s calendar says today, US and UK product pages still say Coming soon. Elsewhere, IKEA’s Matter-over-Thread cabinet driver is on European shelves, and Z-Wave JS UI tips to 11.24.1.

01 · Devices / Hue

Hue Play gradient strip pro — Sep 15 window opens; storefronts still Coming soon

What happened: Signify’s 3 Sep IFA press still calendars the Play gradient strip light / strip light pro portfolio as available from 15 September (from €99.99). This morning, primary Hue storefronts have not flipped: the US pro 100" page ($229.99) and the UK pro 65" page (£139.99) both still show Coming soon. Pro keeps OmniGlow wall-wash and cut-to-fit mounting; Matter for Hue lighting remains via Bridge / third-party hub as before.

Why it matters: Calendar day is real; cart day is not universal yet. Treat this as a rolling ship, not a guaranteed every-SKU drop overnight.

Primary source
Signify press, 3 Sep 2026; Hue US pro 100"; Hue UK pro 65"

02 · Matter & Thread / Devices

IKEA DUBBELKISEL — Matter-over-Thread cabinet driver lists in Europe

What happened: IKEA’s DUBBELKISEL 30 W smart LED driver (article 50609444) is live on European product pages as a Matter-compatible replacement path for the older Zigbee TRÅDFRI wireless driver. Official copy: Matter over Thread, needs a Thread Border Router (DIRIGERA cited as an example), up to 8 lighting units under 30 W total, up to 10 drivers per installation. Germany lists €22.00. Reporting yesterday (14 Sep) put NL/DE among early sale markets; US/UK timing still unannounced.

Why it matters: Cheap, local-first Matter lighting for kitchen/cabinet runs — useful for Home Assistant Thread setups once the SKU hits your market.

Primary source
IKEA DE product page; HomeKit News, 14 Sep 2026

03 · Z-Wave

Z-Wave JS UI 11.24.1 ships

What happened: Today (15 Sep, 11:01 UTC) zwave-js-ui tagged v11.24.1. The release notes are a single feature line: bump @zwave-js/server to 3.10.2 (that server cut landed the same morning and includes refusing new clients while the driver is not ready, plus a fix that captures removed-node state before queueing events). Driver remains on node-zwave-js 15.29.0 (10 Sep).

Why it matters: Small UI/server tip — take it if you already run 11.24.0 and want the server hardening.

Primary source
zwave-js-ui v11.24.1 (published 2026-09-15T11:01:15Z); zwave-js-server 3.10.2
