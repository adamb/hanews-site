Product · 12 Aug 2026

ESPHome ships its first official product

A snap-apart kit on an ESP32-C6, designed and produced by Apollo Automation, the Open Home Foundation’s second commercial partner. Motion, climate, button, and notify modules. Local firmware, no cloud required. Majority of profit to the Foundation.

Explainer

What the ESPHome Starter Kit actually is

Four modules, ribbon cables, a C6 board, and the desktop Device Builder. What it is not: a soldering class, a retailer gadget dump, or a subscription.
