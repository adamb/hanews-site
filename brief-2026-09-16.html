ESPHome 2026.9.0 ships: Noise-encrypted OTA updates and parallelized PlatformIO installs cut package-phase CI time. Elsewhere, Hue’s Play gradient strip pro is still Coming soon a day after the calendar date, and IKEA’s Matter-over-Thread cabinet driver stays on European shelves.

01 · ESPHome

ESPHome 2026.9.0 — faster builds and encrypted updates

What happened: ESPHome tagged stable 2026.9.0 today (GitHub release ~2026-09-16T08:47:11Z; official blog same day). Headline theme: faster builds and encrypted updates. OTA now supports Noise (ChaCha20-Poly1305) encryption, using the same key as API encryption to Home Assistant. PlatformIO install/download is parallelized; package-phase CI benchmark drops from roughly 36% to ~25% of build time. An ESP8266 native toolchain foundation lands dormant (user-facing in 2026.10.0). Six new components: ds1603l, d01, sfa40, mk2pvrouter, snapshot, noise. Zigbee-sourced time platform on ESP32.

Upgrade notes: modbus_controller option renames — custom_command → custom_pdu; register_count / force_new_range → reuse_previous_range. ESP32 custom eFuse MAC is now applied to Ethernet, Bluetooth, and 802.15.4 — devices with a custom MAC may need DHCP / BLE / Thread re-commission.

Why it matters: Encrypted OTA closes a gap for fleets that already encrypt the API; the PlatformIO parallelization is the build-time win most CI users will feel first.

Primary source
ESPHome blog, 16 Sep 2026; esphome/esphome 2026.9.0

IKEA DUBBELKISEL — Matter-over-Thread cabinet driver still listing in Europe

What happened: IKEA’s DUBBELKISEL 30 W smart LED driver (article 50609444) remains live on European product pages as a Matter-compatible replacement path for the older Zigbee TRÅDFRI wireless driver.
